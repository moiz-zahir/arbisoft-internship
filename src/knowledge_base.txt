1. Machine learning is a subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data instead of following explicit rules.
2. A transformer is a neural network architecture built around self-attention, introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need."
3. Large language models are trained to predict the next token in a sequence, and this simple objective gives rise to broad language understanding.
4. Reinforcement learning trains an agent to make decisions by rewarding desired behavior and penalizing undesired behavior.
5. The Turing Test, proposed by Alan Turing in 1950, evaluates whether a machine's behavior is indistinguishable from a human's.
